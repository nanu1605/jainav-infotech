# Personal data map (spec §16, India's DPDP Act)

What the website collects about people, why, where it is kept, who can see it and how long it stays. Update this file whenever a form, collection or third-party service that touches personal data changes. Retention periods are defaults until the legal review (spec §16).

## Collected through the website (Phase 4)

| Data                                                               | Where it comes from                                                                                                                 | Why                                                                | Stored in                                                                       | Who can see it                                                             | Kept for                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Name, email, phone, company, message                               | Contact, start-a-project, waitlist and careers forms (`leads`)                                                                      | To reply and follow up on the request                              | Postgres (`leads`)                                                              | Admin roles: Owner, Support; Editors read leads                            | 24 months after the last activity (deletion job not built yet)                              |

| Contact topic                                                      | Contact form (`leads.details.topic`)                                                                                                | Route the message to the right inbox                               | Postgres (`leads`)                                                              | As above                                                                   | As above                                                                                    |
| Newsletter email                                                   | Newsletter form (`leads`, type `newsletter`)                                                                                        | Newsletter, only after the emailed confirmation (double opt-in)    | Postgres (`leads`)                                                              | As above                                                                   | Until unsubscribed, then as above                                                           |
| Consent record: notice version, time, keyed hash of the IP address | Every lead form, not privacy requests (`leads.consent`)                                                                             | Proof of what the person agreed to                                 | Postgres (`leads`)                                                              | As above                                                                   | With the lead                                                                               |
| Marketing consent: given, time, text version, withdrawn time       | Optional, unticked box on contact and project forms and, for the launch email, on waitlist forms; implied by the newsletter sign-up | Product news emails only to people who agreed                      | Postgres (`leads.marketingConsent`)                                             | As above                                                                   | With the lead                                                                               |
| Unsubscribe time                                                   | Unsubscribe link or one-click header in emails                                                                                      | Stops all marketing to that address (every lead with it)           | Postgres (`leads.unsubscribedAt`)                                               | As above                                                                   | With the lead                                                                               |
| Language, page, referrer, UTM parameters                           | Every lead form, not privacy requests (`leads.locale`, `leads.source`)                                                              | Reply in the person's language; know which page led to the request | Postgres (`leads`)                                                              | As above                                                                   | With the lead                                                                               |
| CV (PDF)                                                           | Careers application / introduction                                                                                                  | Assess the application                                             | Private R2 bucket (`privateFiles`; local disk in development), random file name | Admin roles, through Payload's access check and then a 5-minute signed URL | Deleted with its lead                                                                       |
| Privacy request: email, type, details                              | Privacy request form on `/legal/privacy-requests` (`dataRequests`)                                                                  | To handle DPDP rights requests                                     | Postgres (`data-requests`)                                                      | Owner, Support                                                             | Until handled, then per the legal review                                                    |
| Keyed hash of the IP (IPv6: its /64) and of the email inbox        | Rate limiting of forms and `/go/` links                                                                                             | Abuse protection                                                   | Postgres schema `rate_limit` (HMAC keys only)                                   | Nobody through the app                                                     | About an hour after the limit window ends (swept every 5 minutes), so at most about 2 hours |

The website stores no raw IP addresses, and errors are logged without the submitted data (a failed database write logs only its error code). If the private bucket fails to delete a CV, the lead is still deleted and the failure is only logged (see `docs/decisions.md`, Phase 4). Auto-reply emails never repeat what the visitor typed, so the forms can't be used to send someone else arbitrary text.

## The store (Phase 7)

| Data                                                                                                                                                                                           | Where it comes from                                                                                   | Why                                                         | Stored in                                                                                 | Who can see it                                                                                                                                                                       | Kept for                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Email address, preferred language                                                                                                                                                              | Signing in with an email code (`customers`)                                                           | The account, receipts and invoices                          | Postgres (`customers`)                                                                    | Owner, Support                                                                                                                                                                       | Until the account is deleted, then anonymized                                             |
| Name, phone, billing state, business name, GSTIN                                                                                                                                               | Account settings and checkout                                                                         | Invoices (place of supply, spec §11.4); prefilling checkout | Postgres (`customers`)                                                                    | Owner, Support                                                                                                                                                                       | As above                                                                                  |
| Marketing consent: given, time, text version, withdrawn time                                                                                                                                   | Optional box on the account settings page                                                             | Product news only to people who agreed                      | Postgres (`customers.marketingConsent`)                                                   | Owner, Support                                                                                                                                                                       | With the account                                                                          |
| Sign-in codes                                                                                                                                                                                  | Requesting a code                                                                                     | Signing in                                                  | Postgres (`otp-challenges`): keyed hashes of the email and the code, keyed hash of the IP | Owner (hashes only)                                                                                                                                                                  | 10 minutes; deleted on use, expired ones daily                                            |
| Sessions: token hash, browser user agent, last use                                                                                                                                             | Signing in                                                                                            | Staying signed in (cookie `__Host-jv_session`, essential)   | Postgres (`customer-sessions`); the cookie holds the random token                         | Owner                                                                                                                                                                                | 30 days after the last use; deleted on sign-out and account deletion                      |
| Orders: billing snapshot (name, email, phone, state, business name, GSTIN), amounts, coupon, the Terms and EULA versions accepted with time and keyed IP hash, Razorpay ids, history           | Checkout                                                                                              | The purchase, its proof of acceptance and the tax record    | Postgres (`orders`)                                                                       | Owner, Support                                                                                                                                                                       | Per tax law ({{CONFIRM_WITH_CA}}); kept when the account is deleted                       |
| Invoices: seller and buyer snapshots, lines, totals; the PDF                                                                                                                                   | Fulfilment after payment                                                                              | GST invoice (spec §11.4)                                    | Postgres (`invoices`); PDF in the private R2 bucket (`private-files`)                     | Owner, Support; the customer, through their account                                                                                                                                  | Per tax law; never changed after issue                                                    |
| Licenses: product, plan, customer, order, the key (a SHA-256 hash, an AES-256-GCM encrypted copy and its last four characters), status, update period, internal notes                          | Fulfilment after payment; the Owner issuing a complimentary license                                   | Pro features in the app (spec §12)                          | Postgres (`licenses`)                                                                     | Owner, Support; the customer, through their account (the key)                                                                                                                        | As long as the license exists; kept when the account is deleted, so the app keeps working |
| Activations: a device id hash (SHA-256 of the app's already hashed machine id with `DEVICE_ID_PEPPER`), device name, OS, app version, first and last seen, when and by whom it was deactivated | The VYRO app (`/activate`, `/validate`, `/deactivate`)                                                | The device limit; showing the customer their devices        | Postgres (`activations`)                                                                  | Owner, Support; the customer, through their account; anyone holding the license key sees the active devices' names and last-seen times (the app's `activation_limit_reached` answer) | With the license (no deletion job yet)                                                    |
| Trials: product, device id hash, start and end                                                                                                                                                 | The VYRO app (`/trial`)                                                                               | One trial per device                                        | Postgres (`trials`)                                                                       | Owner, Support                                                                                                                                                                       | Kept, so a device's trial can't restart; nothing else about the device is stored          |
| Razorpay webhook payloads (the payer's email and phone, and the payment method's summary: UPI id, card network, last four digits and issuer, or bank)                                          | Razorpay                                                                                              | Confirming payments, refunds and disputes                   | Postgres (`webhook-events`)                                                               | Owner                                                                                                                                                                                | 90 days after handling (daily cleanup job)                                                |
| Keyed hashes of the IP, email and customer id                                                                                                                                                  | Rate limits on sign-in, account changes, checkout and the licensing API (with the license key's hash) | Abuse protection                                            | Postgres schema `rate_limit`                                                              | Nobody through the app                                                                                                                                                               | As for forms                                                                              |

Card numbers, CVVs, UPI PINs and bank credentials go straight from the browser to Razorpay's checkout and never reach the website; Razorpay's webhooks carry only the summary above. Job records hold only record ids. Order emails go in the order's language and carry the invoice PDF and the license key; complimentary licenses email their key, and a new device activation emails the license's customer (device name, OS, time). The licensing API never receives the raw machine id: the app sends a SHA-256 of it, which the server hashes again with a pepper before storing.

## Not personal data

`dailyStats` download and `/go/` click counters (counts per day and key). The audit log stores only ids for leads, data requests, private files, users, testimonials, customers and activations, and nothing for trials.
