# Personal data map (spec §16, India's DPDP Act)

What the website collects about people, why, where it is kept, who can see it and how long it stays. Update this file whenever a form, collection or third-party service that touches personal data changes. Retention periods are defaults until the legal review (spec §16).

The website's database stores no raw IP addresses. Failed form saves log only the database error code, but other error logs can occasionally include an email address or other details a person entered, such as a name or phone number (see Logs). If the private bucket fails to delete a CV, the lead is still deleted and the failure is only logged (see `docs/decisions.md`, Phase 4). Auto-reply emails never repeat what the visitor typed, so the forms can't be used to send someone else arbitrary text. A leads CSV holds names, emails, phone numbers, messages and consent dates, and sits on the staff member's computer once downloaded.

The only marketing email the code sends is the waitlist email the Owner starts: once per address, to waitlist leads for that product whose consent is given, not withdrawn and not unsubscribed, and which aren't marked spam. Nothing sends a newsletter yet, or news to contact or project leads or to customers who ticked the account box. A ticked form box counts at once, with no email confirmation; only the newsletter uses double opt-in. A newsletter lead's `marketingConsent.given` is true from sign-up, so `confirmedAt` is what shows a confirmed subscriber, and anyone mailing from a CSV must filter on it by hand. A second waitlist sign-up with the box ticked records consent on the existing lead and clears a withdrawal; if that lead has unsubscribed, a new lead is created instead. Unsubscribing sets no withdrawal time and doesn't touch `customers.marketingConsent`, and the account box doesn't touch leads. Staff can record a withdrawal or an unsubscribe on a lead but can't clear one.

Card numbers, CVVs, UPI PINs and bank credentials go straight from the browser to Razorpay's checkout and never reach the website; Razorpay's webhooks carry only the summary above. Job records hold only record ids. Order emails go in the order's language and carry the invoice PDF and the license key; complimentary licenses email their key, and a new device activation emails the license's customer (device name, OS, time). A processed refund emails the order's billing address with the credit note attached (a refunded duplicate payment gets a short note), and a suspended or revoked license emails its customer; deleted customers get none of these. The CA report (Owner) is built on request from invoices and credit notes and not stored: it holds buyer names, business names, states and GSTINs, and goes to the CA from the Owner's computer, so keep it like the invoices. The leads report holds counts only. The app's contract (`docs/licensing-api.md`) is to send a SHA-256 of the machine id and never the raw id; the server can check only the value's format, and hashes it again with a pepper before storing. VYRO v1.4.0 has no licensing code yet.

Deleting an account (its Settings page) replaces the email, clears the name, phone, state, business name and GSTIN, marks a live marketing consent as withdrawn and ends every session. It leaves the customer row with its id, country, language, timestamps and consent record; orders with their billing details, acceptance IP hash and history; invoices and their PDFs; licenses with their key copies and notes, which keep working; activations with their device details; leads and privacy requests with the same email; and webhook payloads until their 90 days end. Signing in again with that address creates a new, empty account. The Owner can't delete a customer with orders or licenses; deleting any other customer removes the row and its sessions.

## Backups

| Data                                                                                                                            | Where it comes from     | Why                      | Stored in                                                      | Who can see it                                                | Kept for                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Encrypted dumps of the `jainav` and `umami` databases: every table above, the `rate_limit` schema, staff accounts and analytics | Nightly job (02:30 IST) | Recovery after a failure | R2 bucket `jainav-backups`, encrypted with age to a public key | Only the holder of the private key, kept offline by the Owner | Daily copies until older than 30 days; monthly copies (the run on the 1st by UTC, so 02:30 IST on the 2nd) until older than 365 days, so 12 or 13 exist |

A row deleted from the live database therefore stays in the dumps for up to about 30 days in the daily copies and about 365 days in the monthly ones, and a Razorpay webhook payload can last about 455 days after receipt (90 live, then up to 365 in a monthly dump). Old copies are pruned only after a successful upload, so a failed run deletes nothing. Restoring a dump brings back rows deleted after it was made, and no code applies those deletions again; a real restore also keeps the replaced database as `jainav_broken` until someone drops it. The monthly restore test decrypts a copy into an in-memory database on the server and removes it afterwards. CVs, invoice PDFs and media live in R2, not in the dumps. Staging has its own database and isn't backed up.

## Retention periods with no deletion job

Only these deletions run on their own: expired sign-in codes and sessions and webhook payloads 90 days after receipt (the daily `cleanup` job), expired `rate_limit` rows (every 5 minutes) and old backup copies (the nightly job). The rest has no deletion job:

| Data                                          | Period                                                                         | What is behind it                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Leads and their CVs                           | Target of 24 months after the last contact                                     | Nothing automatic: no job touches leads and the schema has no last-activity field. Only the Owner can delete a lead, which takes its CV with it |
| Newsletter sign-ups                           | As for leads                                                                   | Nothing automatic: unsubscribing only sets a time, and an unconfirmed sign-up stays after its link stops working                                |
| Privacy requests                              | No fixed period                                                                | Closing a request only records who and when. Only the Owner can delete one                                                                      |
| Orders, invoices and credit notes             | Per tax and company law, currently eight financial years ({{CONFIRM_WITH_CA}}) | No job, and the app lets nobody delete them, so deletion after that is only a target                                                            |
| Licenses, activations and trials              | No end date                                                                    | No job, and the app lets nobody delete them                                                                                                     |
| Customer accounts                             | Until deleted                                                                  | Inactive accounts don't expire, and a deleted account's anonymized row stays                                                                    |
| Staff accounts, team members and testimonials | Until removed                                                                  | Removed by hand                                                                                                                                 |
| Audit log                                     | No end date                                                                    | No job, and the app lets nobody delete entries                                                                                                  |
| Waitlist emails                               | Kept                                                                           | No job; only drafts can be deleted                                                                                                              |
| Analytics (Umami)                             | No end date                                                                    | Umami has no retention setting and no job runs for it                                                                                           |
| Logs                                          | Spec §16 sets 30–90 days; the privacy policy's {{LOG_RETENTION}} is still open | Rotation by size only                                                                                                                           |
| Copies with staff and providers               | No fixed period                                                                | Alert emails in the mailbox, what the other providers hold, and CSV downloads have no retention code here                                       |

## Not personal data

`daily-stats` download and `/go/` click counters (counts per day and key).
