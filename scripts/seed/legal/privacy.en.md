## What we collect and why

Forms and purchases are your choice, but we cannot complete one without what it needs. A "hash" here means a scrambled value we store instead of the original, and a "keyed" hash also needs our secret key to check.

### Forms

Sending a form means you agree to the uses listed for it here. You can withdraw at any time (see Your rights).

- **Contact form:** topic, name, email, message, and optional phone, company and product. Why: to reply and route your message.
- **Project form:** service, description, budget band, timeline, name, email, and optional phone and company. Why: to reply with questions or a first plan.
- **Waitlist form:** email and product. Why: to confirm your sign-up and, only if you tick the box, email you when the product is ready. If we send that email, we keep a hash of your address so that you get it only once.
- **Newsletter form:** email. Why: to send a confirmation link and, once you confirm, news of new releases.
- **Careers form:** name, email, CV (PDF), optional phone, links and message, and any job you applied for. Why: to assess your application and reply.
- **Privacy request form:** email, request type, details, your language, and your account if signed in. We add a ticket number and the dates it is due. Why: to act on your request, reply in your language and keep a record.

The other forms also store your language, the page you were on, the referring page and any campaign tags, so we can reply in your language and see what led to your message. They also store a consent record, to show what you agreed to: the notice version, the time and a keyed hash of your IP address. Staff can add a status and notes.

### Email, phone and WhatsApp

If you email, call or WhatsApp us instead of using a form, we get your address or number and what you tell us. Why: to reply and deal with your request.

### Store account and orders

If you create an account or buy a paid plan:

- **Sign-in:** a one-time code, stored with your email and IP address only as keyed hashes, then a hashed session token, your browser type and last use. Why: to sign you in.
- **Account:** email, language, marketing choice and any name, phone, state, business name and GSTIN you add. Why: to run your account and prepare invoices.
- **Orders and invoices:** billing name, email, state and any phone, business name and GSTIN you add; the plan, amounts, coupon, payment references, refund and dispute history; and the Terms and EULA versions you accepted, with the time and a keyed hash of your IP address. Why: to fulfil orders, handle refunds and disputes, keep tax records and show what you accepted.
- **Payment:** you pay in Razorpay's window. Your card number, CVV, UPI PIN and bank login go to Razorpay, not to us. Razorpay sends us the result with your email, phone and a payment method summary (UPI id, or card network, last four digits and issuer, or bank). Why: to confirm payments, refunds and disputes.

### Licenses and trials

If you hold a license or use a trial through our licensing service:

- **License:** the key (stored as a hash, an encrypted copy and its last four characters), product, plan, status, update period, your account and order, and staff notes. Why: to provide the license, show your key and give support.
- **Devices:** a device identifier (stored only as a hash), device name, operating system, app version, and first-seen, last-seen and deactivation times. Why: to apply the device limit, show your devices and email you about new activations. Anyone with your key who reaches the limit can see your active devices' names and last-seen times, so keep it private.
- **Trials:** product, hashed device identifier, and start and end dates. Why: to allow one trial per device.

### Technical data

- **Spam check:** Cloudflare Turnstile loads without a click when a form nears your screen, which can happen on any page. Cloudflare gets your IP address and browser signals, and also uses them to improve its own bot detection. Why: to block bots.
- **Abuse limits:** keyed hashes of your IP address and sometimes your email, account or license key. Why: to stop repeated attempts.
- **Server logs:** the time, result and full page address of each request, without your IP address or browser details. Error logs can occasionally include an email address or other details you entered, such as a name or phone number. Why: to run the site and fix faults.
- **Analytics:** when switched on, Umami, on our own server, records full page addresses, referring pages, your browser, device, language and approximate location, and actions such as sending a form, not what you typed. Your IP address is used for location and for an identifier that changes monthly, and Umami's database does not store it. Why: to see how the site is used.
- **Error reports:** when switched on, Sentry receives technical details of errors, the page address, cut at any question mark, your browser type, language and time zone, and a record with a random id for each page load. It sees your IP address, and we ask it not to store that. We do not filter error messages, so one could include something you typed. Why: to find and fix faults.

For cookies and browser storage, see the [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies).

## How long we keep it

- **Form data and CVs:** our target is deletion within 24 months of our last contact with you, done by hand, not by an automatic job. Unsubscribing does not delete your record.
- **Privacy requests:** kept as a record, with no fixed period or automatic deletion yet.
- **Sign-in codes, sessions and abuse limits:** codes last 10 minutes, sessions about 30 days after last use or until you sign out, and abuse-limit records about two hours. Automatic jobs remove them.
- **Store account:** your details until you delete the account, then a basic record with no end date. Inactive accounts do not expire.
- **Orders, invoices and credit notes:** as long as tax and company law require, currently eight financial years {{CONFIRM_WITH_CA}}. Deletion after that is a target, not an automatic job.
- **Razorpay's payment messages:** a daily job deletes them 90 days after receipt.
- **License, device and trial records:** no end date at present, so a license keeps working and a device cannot restart a trial.
- **Server logs:** {{LOG_RETENTION}}.
- **Analytics:** no end date at present.
- **Backups:** encrypted nightly database copies, 30 daily and about 12 monthly. Deleted data can stay in a backup for up to about a year, and restoring one could bring it back.
- **Other copies:** staff alert emails, staff downloads, and emails and WhatsApp messages you send us have no fixed period, and providers set their own periods.

## Security

- Connections to the site use HTTPS.
- Our admin area needs a staff login and a separate Cloudflare check.
- CVs and invoice PDFs are in private storage, and staff reach them through links that last 5 minutes.
- Backups are encrypted, with the key kept offline.
- Access controls protect the live database.

We will tell you and the authorities about a personal data breach as the law requires.

## Your rights

You can:

- **Ask what we hold:** a summary of your data, what we do with it and who received it. The form calls this "Get a copy of my data".
- **Correct or complete it:** we can change account details and add a correcting note to form data. We do not edit issued invoices or orders.
- **Ask us to delete it:** we do, unless we still need it for its purpose or the law requires us to keep it. Our system cannot currently delete orders, invoices, licenses, device records or trial records.
- **Delete a store account:** on its Settings page. This replaces your email, clears your name, phone, state, business name and GSTIN, withdraws marketing consent and signs you out everywhere. If you cannot use that page, ask us: we make the same changes for you. We keep your orders and invoices with their billing details, licenses and keys (which keep working), device records, form data, privacy requests and a basic account record.
- **Withdraw consent:** at any time. We then stop that use within a reasonable time, unless the law requires or allows it to continue. Earlier use stands, we still complete what you have paid for, and a service that needs that data may have to stop.
- **Name someone to act for you** if you die or cannot act yourself.

To do any of this, or raise a grievance, use the form on [Privacy Requests and Grievances](/legal/privacy-requests) or contact the Grievance Officer. Give the email address you used with us. We answer rights requests within one month.

You can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, once that route is in force. The Act asks you to raise a grievance with us first. Nothing in this policy limits that right.
