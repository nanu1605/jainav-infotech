## Paid plans

If you buy a paid plan, you get a license key and download the app from this website. Once your payment is confirmed, which usually takes a few seconds:

- The confirmation page shows your key, invoice link and download button.
- We email the key and invoice PDF to your account's email address.

Your account also keeps them: the Licenses page has the key, Orders & invoices has the invoice, and Downloads has the app.

## If your key has not arrived

Some payments take longer to confirm. If yours does, the page tells you, and you can then close it. The email follows once the payment is confirmed.

If you still have no key:

1. Check your spam folder.
2. Sign in and open your account's Licenses page. The key is there once the payment is confirmed, even if the email failed. We retry failed emails, but you get no notice of a failure.
3. If it is not there, or you cannot sign in, [contact us](/contact?topic=support) with your order number, or the date and amount of the payment.

We can send the key again while your license is active and your account has not been deleted. If you paid and did not receive what you bought, you can get a fix or a refund: see [Refunds and Cancellation](/legal/refunds-cancellation).
