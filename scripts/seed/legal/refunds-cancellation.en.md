In short: ask within {{REFUND_WINDOW_DAYS}} days of purchase and we refund you for any reason. After that, we fix or refund anything defective, deficient, not genuine, not as described or agreed, not delivered or delivered late. Nothing here takes away a right the law gives you, including under the Consumer Protection Act.

## Who this page covers

It applies whether you buy for yourself or for a business. It does not cover project work we agree separately, or anything bought on a product's own website, such as [menumesh.in](https://menumesh.in). For a purchase on a product's own website, follow that website's policy, or [contact us](/contact?topic=support) if you cannot find it.

Free downloads and anything else we give you free involve no payment, so there is nothing to refund. If a free app gives you trouble, [tell us](/contact?topic=support).

## Cancelling before you pay

Until your payment goes through, there is nothing to cancel: just leave the checkout. An unpaid order lapses after 24 hours, without an email. If a payment still goes through later, we complete the order and the rest of this page applies. If money left your account but your order did not complete, Razorpay can return it by itself. [Write to us](/contact?topic=support) with the date and amount, and we will check.

We charge no cancellation fee. Once you have paid, cancelling means asking for a refund.

## Money back for any reason

If you ask within {{REFUND_WINDOW_DAYS}} days of purchase, we refund what you paid, for any reason, even if you have already activated your key. We count from the date on your order and invoice, in India time.

## Cases we always put right

After that window, we will still put it right if the license or software is:

- defective or deficient
- not genuine
- not as described or not as agreed
- not delivered, or delivered late (see [how delivery works](/legal/shipping-delivery))

We can fix the problem or refund you. If we cannot fix it within a reasonable time, or the fix does not work, we refund you. Where the law lets you choose a refund instead of a fix, the choice is yours.

A duplicate payment is always refunded. If it is on the same order, the order and license stay as they are, and we email you once it is done. There is no credit note, because that payment has no invoice. If it created a second order, we refund that order as below, and only its license is affected.

## How to ask

Use the [contact page](/contact?topic=support), email [support@jainavinfotech.in](mailto:support@jainavinfotech.in) or reply to your order email. Give your order number, which starts with JO-. If you do not have it, give the date and amount of the payment.

We aim to answer within 4 working days, and never later than one month.

## What happens next

We handle each request by hand, and our system starts no refund on its own.

The money goes back to your original payment method through Razorpay, our checkout's payment service. We start the refund within 7 days of approving it, and banks usually take 5 to 10 working days more. If the refund is for a case we always put right, we aim to get the money to you within 30 days of your request. {{CONFIRM_WITH_LAWYER}} If the original method can no longer take it, we arrange another route with you.

When Razorpay confirms the refund has been processed:

- We issue a credit note that refers to your invoice, which itself does not change.
- We email you the refund amount and the credit note as a PDF. It also stays with the order in your account.
- A partial refund leaves your license as it is.
- Once refunds reach the order's full total, we revoke its license, and that email tells you. The key can then no longer be activated, and your right to the paid plan ends. Free versions of the app and open source code stay under their own licenses.

If a refund fails, we are alerted, but you get no credit note or automatic email. We try again or arrange another route. If the money has not arrived in the time above, write to us.

We do not send the automatic emails on this page once you have deleted your account, so ask us for copies.

## Payment disputes and chargebacks

Nothing on this page stops you raising a dispute or chargeback with your bank.

When a dispute opens on a paid order's payment, the order shows as Disputed in your account, and we suspend its active license and email you. A suspended key cannot be activated. A dispute on a duplicate payment changes neither the order nor the license.

Later stages, including the end of the dispute, change nothing by themselves. The license stays suspended until we reinstate or revoke it. If the dispute ends with your money returned, we can revoke it. We email you if we revoke it, but not if we reinstate it, so check your account's Licenses page. The order can keep showing Disputed after the dispute ends, unless its full total is refunded.
