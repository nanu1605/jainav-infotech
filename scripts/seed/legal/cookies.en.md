We are Jainav Infotech Private Limited. This page lists the cookies (small pieces of data a website asks your browser to keep) and similar browser storage on this website.

If you only read our pages or send a form, this website sets no cookie of its own. It sets cookies only for people who sign in. Other companies can set their own, as described below.

## Cloudflare

All traffic to this website passes through Cloudflare, which helps protect it. Depending on our security settings and what it detects, Cloudflare may set security cookies such as `__cf_bm` (bot detection), `_cfuvid` (rate limiting) or `cf_clearance` (proof that your browser passed a check).

Our forms and sign-in use Cloudflare Turnstile as a spam check. It loads without a click when a form nears your screen, and our page footers hold a form, so this can happen on any page. Turnstile may use its own cookies or browser storage.

## Analytics and error reports

Umami (analytics, on our own server) and Sentry (error reports) set no cookies, and Sentry stores nothing in your browser. When switched on, both still receive details of your visit, as our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) explains.

## Store accounts

If you sign in to a store account, `__Host-jv_session` keeps you signed in. It holds a random token that page scripts cannot read, and it travels only over secure connections. Your browser may keep it for up to 400 days, but your sign-in ends about 30 days after you last use the account. Signing out or deleting the account removes it from that browser. While our store is closed, nobody can sign in, so it is not set.

## Our staff

Staff who sign in to our admin area also get:

- `payload-token`, which keeps them signed in for 2 hours at a time.
- `payload-lng` and `payload-theme`, which remember the admin language and theme for up to a year.
- `__prerender_bypass`, for previewing unpublished pages, until they exit the preview or close the browser.
- Cookies from Cloudflare Access, which guards the admin area, such as `CF_Authorization`, `CF_AppSession`, `CF_Session` and `CF_Device`, lasting from 4 hours to 30 days.

The admin area also stores `_payloadClipboard` in local storage and `payload-pending-success-toast` and `payload-forgot-password:users` in session storage.

## Settings kept in your browser

Our pages can keep these in your browser's local storage until you clear them:

- `theme`: light or dark, saved when you click the theme button.
- `jv-lang-pref`: `en` or `hi`, saved when you pick a language or answer the suggestion to read in Hindi.
- `jv-announcement-dismissed`: the announcement you closed, so it stays closed.

## Other companies' services

These companies may set their own cookies or storage, under their own policies. We do not control or list them.

- Google Maps loads only after you click **Show map** on the [contact page](/contact).
- A product page may embed a video or calendar from YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, Cal.com or Calendly. If it does, the embed loads as it nears your screen, without a click.
- If you buy a paid plan, Razorpay's checkout loads after you press Pay.
- Booking links (Cal.com or Calendly), WhatsApp buttons, share buttons and other links to outside websites load nothing until you click them.

## Your choices

This website has no cookie settings panel. You can block or delete cookies and stored data in your browser's settings. Most pages should still work, but you cannot sign in without `__Host-jv_session`, forms will not send if Turnstile is blocked, and your theme is forgotten. A page's language comes from its address, so this does not change it, but after you delete stored data we can suggest Hindi again. You can still reach us at [support@jainavinfotech.in](mailto:support@jainavinfotech.in).

Umami and Sentry use no cookies, so blocking cookies does not stop them. A browser tool that blocks their scripts can stop what your browser sends them, and Umami also stops counting your visit if you set `umami.disabled` in your browser's local storage.
