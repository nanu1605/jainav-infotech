## What you can ask for

- Access: a summary of your data, what we do with it and who received it. The form calls this **Get a copy of my data**.
- Correction: a change to data that is wrong, incomplete or out of date.
- Deletion: removal of what we no longer need, unless the law requires us to keep it.
- Withdrawing consent: stopping a use of your data that you agreed to.
- Grievance: a complaint about how we handled your data or your request, or about something you bought.
- Nomination: naming someone to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself.

Some records cannot be edited or deleted, and backups can keep deleted data for up to about a year. Our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) has the details, including what stays if you delete a store account.

## What happens after you ask

1. A form request gets an automatic confirmation email with a ticket number. If it does not arrive, check your spam folder or email us.
2. A member of our staff finds your data by the email address you used with us and checks that the request is yours. Before we act, we may ask you to confirm from that address, or to give a detail such as an order number.
3. We answer by email. If we cannot do all that you asked, we tell you why.

## How long it takes

- Requests: we answer within one month of receiving them.
- Grievances: we acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within one month of receiving them. Our acknowledgement gives you a ticket number, so you can ask about its status. Our Grievance Officer's first reply includes a copy of your complaint as we recorded it.
- Refund and other purchase questions: we aim to answer within 4 working days, and never later than one month. See [Refunds and Cancellation](/legal/refunds-cancellation).

## Naming someone to act for you

Choose **Name someone to act for me** in the form, or email us. Give your name, the email address you used with us, and the name and contact details of each person you name. Before acting on a nomination, we ask that person to show who they are and what has happened.

## Withdrawing consent and unsubscribing

You can withdraw consent at any time. We then stop that use within a reasonable time, unless the law requires or allows it to continue. Earlier use stands, we still complete what you have paid for, and a service that needs that data may have to stop.

- Marketing emails: use the unsubscribe link in the email. It works at once, for every sign-up we hold for that address at that moment. A later sign-up counts as new. You still get replies and emails about your orders and licenses.
- Store account: untick the marketing box in your account settings. This and the unsubscribe link are separate.
- Anything else, or a link that fails: choose **Withdraw my consent** in the form or email us. Our staff handle it by hand.

## If you are not satisfied

Nothing on this page limits these routes:

- For personal data: complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, once that route is in force. The Act asks you to raise a grievance with us first.
- For something you bought: call the National Consumer Helpline on 1915, or complain to a consumer commission, including where you live or work, online through [e-Jagriti](https://e-jagriti.gov.in).
